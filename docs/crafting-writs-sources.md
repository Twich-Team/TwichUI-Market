# Crafting Writs source and coverage

The catalogue comes from the [AllTheThings Forever export through GitHub's Contents API](https://github.com/ATTWoWAddon/AllTheThings/blob/master/.contrib/.db/forever/expansion%20features/craftsman%27s%20writs.lua). The import reads each exact quest ID, starting writ item ID, delivery item ID and count. It validates the export against its Git blob ID and verifies that every writ belongs to the corresponding tier in the published envelope groups. AllTheThings is [MIT licensed](https://github.com/ATTWoWAddon/AllTheThings/blob/master/LICENSE). Market imports the facts into its own catalogue shape.

The source currently contains 150 writs across five professions, with 45 Journeyman, 60 Expert and 45 Artisan. All 150 starting item links agree with the [Forever 1.60.1.71091 ItemSparse export](https://wago.tools/db2/ItemSparse/csv?build=1.60.1.71091). That build shows the deliveries as unbound or bind-on-equip. The importer also checks the [ForeverChanges crafting quest index](https://foreverchanges.pro/quests/crafting) for faction restrictions. It applies a source's explicit restriction and rejects a refresh if the two sources disagree on the required faction. Crafted Solid Shot requires 200 items. The rest of the current export requires one item per writ.

Blizzard's Forever quest API returned exact quest IDs, daily status and base reputation in sampled responses. Those responses omit structured delivery IDs and quantities. [ForeverDB's API](https://foreverdb.net/developers) documents quest starters, finishers, and links. Its documented quest response does not include structured deliveries. The separate pricing and reputation settings continue to work with the imported catalogue.

## Refresh and fallback

The enrichment worker fetches the writ export and faction index at startup when due, then daily. An administrator can queue an earlier refresh. The worker retries failed responses after at least 15 minutes and respects a longer upstream retry delay. It commits a replacement atomically only after validating the complete import. Readers use the last successful catalogue during retries. They can also use the previous release's cached catalogue until the first successful import. No per-writ data ships in the application. A new installation needs its first successful import before showing writs.

A successful catalogue refresh makes the exact item IDs eligible for the standard ForeverDB metadata backfill. That job uses the existing credential, quota, leases and retries. Market continues to use its own prices. Catalogue refreshes never write administrator reward settings.

## Retained recipe evidence

The source used for the catalogue does not describe craft eligibility, recipe inputs or outputs. The importer retains existing recipe evidence only when both the quest and its exact deliveries still match. Missing recipe evidence means the buy route remains available and the craft route stays unverified. Published tier-wide reputation values are not applied to individual writs by this import.

Recipe 11450 makes one item 8951 from one each of 3355, 3821 and 3372. This is recorded from [Forever client build 1.60.1.70532](https://wowforeverhelper.com/item/8951/elixir-of-defense), independently corroborated by [client build 1.60.1.70755](https://wowforever.ninja/professions/alchemy/11450-elixir-of-greater-defense/). That client evidence predates launch. [GoldCap](https://goldcap.gg/forever/recipes/11450) reports addon observations of Forever trainers teaching it at Alchemy 195. The player must still know the recipe and meet its skill requirement. The comparison relies on the player to confirm eligibility. Names, icons and prices come from the normal item metadata and pricing systems.

[ForeverDB engineering](https://foreverdb.net/fr/profession/engineering?p=1) lists Crafted Solid Shot in batches of 200 from one Heavy Blasting Powder and one Bronze Bar, and marks the trainer as seen in Forever. Its 125 learning skill is labelled Classic. [GoldCap](https://goldcap.gg/forever/recipes/3947) identifies spell 3947 and reports addon observations of Forever trainers teaching it at skill 125. Crafting remains a user-confirmed scenario requiring the learned recipe.

[ForeverDB blacksmithing](https://foreverdb.net/fr/profession/blacksmithing?p=2) gives Big Bronze Knife ingredients and requires a Blacksmith Hammer and anvil; [GoldCap](https://goldcap.gg/forever/recipes/3491) identifies spell 3491. Trainer skill differs across sources: ForeverDB labels its 100 as Classic, while GoldCap reports Forever addon observations at 80. The current observed value is used, and the player must confirm recipe knowledge and meet the tool and station requirements.

Big Bronze Bomb is a useful limitation: [ForeverDB engineering](https://foreverdb.net/es/profession/engineering?p=2) identifies 2–4 output, not a guaranteed 3, with two Heavy Blasting Powder, three Bronze Bars and one Silver Contact. [Wowhead Forever spell 3950](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=3950/big-bronze-bomb) identifies the spell and tools. The random-output route is excluded from guaranteed crafting estimates.
