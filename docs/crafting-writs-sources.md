# Crafting Writs source and coverage

The initial curated subset contains nine buyable-delivery writs across five professions, including **Craftsman's Writ: Elixir of Defense**. [Wowhead's Forever item 264018](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=264018/craftsmans-writ-elixir-of-defense) links to [quest 94218](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=94218/craftsmans-writ-elixir-of-defense), whose objectives require the writ and one [Elixir of Defense (8951)](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=8951/elixir-of-defense). The item page identifies the writ as Journeyman. No arithmetic relationship between item and quest IDs is assumed.

[ForeverDB's guide](https://foreverdb.net/guide/waylaid-crates-and-trade-reputation) says a profession is not needed for delivery, and buying the item works; the writ and item must be carried. It identifies Azeroth Commerce Authority and Durotar Supply and Logistics as the faction-specific reputation recipients. Its 75/125/200 rewards and daily-repeat rule are explicitly speculative. They are not seeded; rewards require administration or stronger provider observations. Legacy bonuses and coin pouches are excluded.

Recipe 11450 makes one item 8951 from one each of 3355, 3821 and 3372. This is recorded from [Forever client build 1.60.1.70532](https://wowforeverhelper.com/item/8951/elixir-of-defense), independently corroborated by [client build 1.60.1.70755](https://wowforever.ninja/professions/alchemy/11450-elixir-of-greater-defense/). That client evidence predates launch. [GoldCap](https://goldcap.gg/forever/recipes/11450) reports addon observations of Forever trainers teaching it at Alchemy 195. The player must still know the recipe and meet its skill requirement; this is a user scenario, not a verified character capability. Names, icons and prices come from the normal item metadata and pricing systems.

[ForeverDB's documented API](https://foreverdb.net/developers) offers item, quest and profession resources, but does not document a writ catalogue or a quest-objective relationship feed. The seed is therefore a pinned local observation, not a claimed successful ForeverDB catalogue refresh. Provider outages do not remove it. No whole-provider crawl, inferred ID offset, unverified faction-specific mapping, or assumed reward is used. Other writs and possible alternate recipes are excluded pending exact response-backed relationships. The guide reports 150 writs, so coverage is intentionally small. No automatic catalogue refresh cadence is claimed until an adequate source exists.

Other individually linked Forever item → quest → delivery identities are [Big Bronze Knife](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=94227/craftsmans-writ-big-bronze-knife), [Big Bronze Bomb](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=94236/craftsmans-writ-big-bronze-bomb), [Crafted Solid Shot](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=94237/craftsmans-writ-crafted-solid-shot) (200 required), [Heavy Leather Ball](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=94238/craftsmans-writ-heavy-leather-ball), [Barbaric Gloves](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=94245/craftsmans-writ-barbaric-gloves), and [Colorful Kilt](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=94253/craftsmans-writ-colorful-kilt). Their quest pages link both their writ and delivery item. Profession assignments are corroborated by [Wowhead's Forever writ guide](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/professions/craftsmans-writs-overview-recipes-rewards). Their recipe data is not yet established.

The worker schedules a finite item metadata backfill at most daily when the existing ForeverDB provider is enabled with an unexpired validated credential; an administrator can also queue it. Both use existing quotas, leases and retries. This refreshes item metadata only, not the curated quest relationships. A bounded authenticated quest probe attempted on 2026-11-25 was deferred by the existing shared provider budget before any request was made.

Additional tier examples: [Fire Protection Potion](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=94263/craftsmans-writ-fire-protection-potion) (Expert, item 264063, delivery 6049) and [Greater Fire Protection Potion](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=94318/craftsmans-writ-greater-fire-protection-potion) (Artisan, item 264118, delivery 13457). Quest pages link exact items and require one of each delivery.

[ForeverDB item 8951](https://foreverdb.net/item/8951) identifies the current Forever client build 1.60.1.71091 as crafting the elixir with Alchemy. It also reports auction listings for item 8951 and [item 8069](https://foreverdb.net/item/8069), supporting their tradeability; no listing prices are imported into this catalogue.

[ForeverDB engineering](https://foreverdb.net/fr/profession/engineering?p=1) lists Crafted Solid Shot in batches of 200 from one Heavy Blasting Powder and one Bronze Bar, and marks the trainer as seen in Forever. Its 125 learning skill is labelled Classic; independently, [GoldCap](https://goldcap.gg/forever/recipes/3947) identifies spell 3947 and reports addon observations of Forever trainers teaching it at skill 125. Crafting remains a user-confirmed scenario requiring the learned recipe.

[ForeverDB blacksmithing](https://foreverdb.net/fr/profession/blacksmithing?p=2) gives Big Bronze Knife ingredients and requires a Blacksmith Hammer and anvil; [GoldCap](https://goldcap.gg/forever/recipes/3491) identifies spell 3491. Trainer skill differs across sources: ForeverDB labels its 100 as Classic, while GoldCap reports Forever addon observations at 80. The current observed value is used, and the player must confirm recipe knowledge and meet the tool and station requirements.

Big Bronze Bomb is a useful limitation: [ForeverDB engineering](https://foreverdb.net/es/profession/engineering?p=2) identifies 2–4 output, not a guaranteed 3, with two Heavy Blasting Powder, three Bronze Bars and one Silver Contact. [Wowhead Forever spell 3950](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=3950/big-bronze-bomb) identifies the spell and tools. The random-output route is excluded from guaranteed crafting estimates.
